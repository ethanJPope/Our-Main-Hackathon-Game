## Reusable prompt

Primary request: Create a completely new, original stylized low-poly painterly fantasy forest environment for a landscape mobile isometric adventure. It is a lush handcrafted miniature wilderness that feels cozy, quietly magical, inviting to explore, and slightly mysterious. Preserve the game's established lore and gameplay needs while discarding the visual theme of V1–V9.

Scene variation: `[LOCATION]`, featuring `[LANDMARK]`, viewed from `[CAMERA ANGLE]`.

Geometry: Use chunky irregular handcrafted low-poly geometry with visible broad facets and strong readable silhouettes. Build conifers from overlapping downward-hanging tapered polygonal branch masses made of broad shallow ribbon-like slabs, with irregular lengths and offsets forming triangular silhouettes. Build grass from dense clumps of broad squared flat ribbon blades with varied heights, mostly upright forms, a few gentle bends, and small ground gaps. Add broad leafy ground-cover masses, beveled irregular stone slabs, mossy roots, and sparse tiny purple, pale cyan-white, yellow, and warm orange-brown accents. Construct each fallen log as one simple roughly squared trunk made from 2–3 joined low-sided masses, with 4–6 broad visible planes, a slight angular kink or taper, solid uneven angled ends, 2–3 broad orange-brown value regions, and only 1–2 thin flat moss shapes on top.

Materials: Use matte, nonmetallic, high-roughness materials with restrained specular response. Add broad hand-painted tonal variation and subtle per-face color differences. Keep texture low frequency and subordinate to the geometry. The result feels intentionally modeled and painted by hand, never procedurally perfect.

Palette: Deep teal-green shadows, muted evergreen midtones, moss green, olive green, and selective yellow-chartreuse highlights. Highlights become warmer and yellower; shadows become cooler and bluer. Cyan-white beacon energy remains a restrained focal accent.

Lighting: Broad warm sunlight filters through the canopy in irregular dappled patches. Use cool green-blue ambient fill, soft shadows, strong contact occlusion, gentle atmospheric haze, restrained bloom, filmic color grading, and subtle gameplay depth of field. Keep the midground and all traversal boundaries crisp; soften only cropped foreground vegetation and far edges.

Composition: Use a 16:9 high 3/4 top-down isometric-inspired camera at approximately 35-degree elevation, 40-degree field of view, and mild perspective compression. Show no horizon. Fill the frame like a physical fantasy diorama without a floating base. Use layered depth, asymmetrical natural clustering, cropped foreground vegetation, clear visual-rest areas, an easy-to-read landmark, and open traversal spaces suitable for mobile gameplay.

Avoid: realistic bark, wood grain, hollow log ends, growth rings, splinters, dense log moss, individual leaves or needles, fine textures, smooth rounded forms, glossy plastic, metallic ordinary surfaces, outlines, voxel geometry, perfect cubes, repetitive tree rows, sterile procedural symmetry, excessive flowers, clutter, heavy bloom, near-black global gloom, excessive fog, photorealism, a visible horizon, a floating diorama base, text, UI, HUD, logos, watermarks, and recognizable designs from existing games.
